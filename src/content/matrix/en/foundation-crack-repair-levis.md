Across the plateau in Lévis, most footing cracks are settled by injection from inside the
basement, work that touches neither the lot nor the street. It is the most common starting point,
and the simplest to authorise: unlike a drain replacement, it typically requires no municipal
permit at all.

## What stays indoors, and what moves to the yard

A stable crack at the attached-garage joint is treated without ever opening the ground: the
contractor assesses the crack, then injects from the basement. The file changes shape the moment
a second sign shows up — a door that binds, a floor out of level — because that calls for checking
what is happening outside the wall, which means excavating at least one section. Lévis's geography
matters here. A bungalow lot on the Terrasse de Lévis, built between 1970 and 2000, usually leaves
enough clearance on all four sides for that kind of targeted check. An older Vieux-Lévis lot, near
the traverse Québec-Lévis, tends to offer less room, and a contractor may need to work section by
section rather than opening a full stretch of wall at once.

## One city, one line, two different files

Lévis has run its own territory since merging thirteen former municipalities, with its own 311 —
separate from Québec's, which serves La Cité-Limoilou across the river. For a simple interior
injection, that call is usually not needed. It becomes one the moment excavation touches a
property line or comes near a municipal line, whether that is near boulevard Guillaume-Couture,
in Saint-Romuald or in Charny. Asking before the work starts heads off an administrative surprise
later, whether the house sits on the plateau or in Vieux-Lévis.

## What a good quote spells out

Two quotes priced the same for a garage-joint crack do not necessarily cover the same work. One
may include injection alone; another, a targeted exterior opening to confirm the drain's condition
before closing it back up. Ask which of the two is planned, under what condition the second
replaces the first, and whether the lot allows access without negotiating with a neighbour.
